GUESSWORK.

Start with your current CS2 sensitivity and DPI. SENS/LAB compares five nearby settings—from 80% to 120% of your current sensitivity—while keeping your DPI fixed. At each setting, you complete four aim exercises and a 20-second tracking run. We score hit rate, speed, aim error, corrections, and how close you keep the crosshair to the moving target; each exercise contributes equally to the comparison.

The highest-scoring setting is a data-backed starting point for your own practice, not a promise of perfect aim. We also show eDPI (DPI × sensitivity) and estimated cm/360° so you can understand how each setting changes your mouse movement. Your DPI stays the same throughout.
